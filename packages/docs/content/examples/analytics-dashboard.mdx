## How it works

Four independent `<ChartCanvas>` components share the same `syncId`, so hovering one moves the crosshair (and the legend values) in all of them, and `syncRange` keeps their visible range in step while you pan or zoom any chart. The grid is plain CSS: one column on small screens, two on wide ones.
