## BrickDatum

A brick / box produced by the renko or line break transforms (one datum per brick):
`{ date, open, close, fullyFormed? }`. A brick is "up" when close &gt;= open. `fullyFormed: false`
marks the last, still forming renko brick. Extra fields (high, low, volume, from, to...) are ignored.

## KagiDatum

One vertical kagi line, as produced by the kagi transform. The line runs from
`open` to `close`; it starts as `startAs` and, when it breaks the previous peak / trough, switches
to `changeTo` at price `changePoint`. Consecutive lines are joined by a horizontal shoulder / waist at
the next line's `open` (= this line's `close`). Extra fields (high, low, volume, current, reverseAt...) are ignored.

## Lod

Level-of-detail index for large series: min / max (and their indices) plus the first / last valid index of
every block of BLOCK bars. Lets drawing and autoscale work per block instead of per bar when many bars share a
pixel, so the cost of a frame depends on the chart width rather than on the size of the data.

## PointAndFigureDatum

One point & figure column, as produced by the point & figure transform.
`direction` 1 is a column of X (rising), -1 a column of O (falling); `boxes` lists its boxes from the
first to the last, each spanning `open` → `close` (one box size). `open` / `close` are the first box's
open and the last box's close; `high` / `low` default to their max / min. Extra fields are ignored.
