## Resolution

Bar interval in the common charting notation: "1S"/"5S" seconds, "1"/"5"/"15"/"60"/"240" minutes,
"1D" days, "1W" weeks, "1M" months. Friendly aliases are accepted too: "1s", "1m", "1h", "4h", "1d", "1w", "1y".

```ts
type Resolution =
    | "1S"
    | "5S"
    | "15S"
    | "30S"
    | "1"
    | "3"
    | "5"
    | "15"
    | "30"
    | "45"
    | "60"
    | "120"
    | "180"
    | "240"
    | "1D"
    | "1W"
    | "1M"
    | "3M"
    | "6M"
    | "12M"
    | (string & {});
```
