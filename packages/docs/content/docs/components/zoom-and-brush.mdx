## ZoomButtons

`<ZoomButtons>` adds zoom out, zoom in, scroll left / right and reset buttons at the bottom of the chart, shown while
the pointer is over it (`autoHide`). Place it inside `<ChartCanvas>`.

## Brush

`<Brush>` captures drags to select a range: `mode="x"` selects a time range over the full pane height, `"xy"` a
rectangle. By default it zooms into the selection; `onSelect` receives the range (x values, data indices and prices).
Toggle `enabled` to switch between panning and brushing.
