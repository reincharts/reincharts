## Multi-chart layouts

`layout` shows several charts in one widget: `"single"`, `"2h"` (side by side), `"2v"` (stacked), `"3"` (one big + two)
or `"4"` (2 × 2). Users pick it from the toolbar; control it with `layout` + `onLayoutChange`, or set it in the state.
The symbols of the other charts are `state.cells`.

```tsx
<TradingChart datafeed={feed} layout="4" defaultState={{ cells: ["ACME", "GLOBX", "BTCD"] }} syncRange />
```

Crosshairs are always synced between the charts of a layout; `syncRange` also syncs panning and zooming. Click a chart
to make it active: the toolbar, legends and drawing tools act on the active chart.

To sync charts outside a single widget, use `syncId` on [`<ChartCanvas>`](/docs/guides/syncing-charts).

## Bar replay

The replay button in the toolbar opens bar replay: pick a starting bar on the chart, then play, pause,
step and change the speed. Indicators and drawings update as bars appear. Disable it with `replay={false}`. For your own
replay UI, use [`useBarReplay`](/docs/datafeed#bar-replay).
